## Platform

web

## Users
One person: the owner of a fitness-supplements shop in India that sells only through WhatsApp and Instagram (protein bars, whey, shakers, gym gloves). No staff, no ERP. He opens Nivara on a laptop or phone, between customer chats, to answer one question: what needs me today?

## Product Purpose
Tell the owner, every day, three things: which orders to deliver, what to restock before it runs out, and whether a supplier is overcharging. Success: he acts on today's list in a minute and never learns about a stockout from a customer.

## Positioning
Facts come from his own database and deterministic code (stock maths, stockout rules, supplier thresholds); the language model only phrases and extracts, and every answer shows where it came from. Customer and margin data stays on hardware he owns (local Gemma).

## Operating Context
- Orders arrive as chat messages ("Rahul wants 3 chocolate bars and one shaker, deliver tomorrow"), pasted in and confirmed.
- Daily brief generated by a scheduled workflow (08:00 IST).
- Used in short glances; phone use is common (inferred from "between chats").
- Money is rupees (₹, Indian digit grouping, e.g. ₹1,898).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Views: Today, Ask (text + voice), Orders (message extraction), Stock, Forecast (TabPFN, labelled fallback), Suppliers (stored quotes, live prices, memory), Workflows, Activity (traces), Health.
- Order IDs are shown as "Order 4"; the raw `_id` (e.g. `O004`) is kept for API calls.
- Supplier saving counts only at ≥ ₹10 and ≥ 5% per unit; smaller savings are shown as not counted.
- Frontend: plain HTML + JS, no build step, no framework. Every number comes from the API.
- Not built: auth, payments, WhatsApp integration, mobile app.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Nivara.
- Voice: plain shopkeeper English. "3 orders to deliver today", not "16 things need your attention." No "AI-powered", "copilot", "magic", "seamless".
- Honest labelling of fallbacks and demo data is part of the brand.

## Evidence on Hand
Seeded demo data only (`demo: true`): ~15 SKUs, 5 customers, sample orders and supplier quotes. No real customers, testimonials or metrics; do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Today first: the screen answers "what do I do now" before anything else.
2. Show the source: every recommendation says why and where the number came from.
3. Rupees and counts, not scores: money and quantities are the language.
4. Glanceable on a phone between chats.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion respected, usable at 375px. English UI; Hinglish input accepted in order extraction.
